Privacy policy

Your trip stays on your phone.

Kippu plans your train journeys in Japan on your phone. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. This policy explains what Kippu keeps, the little that leaves your phone, and who receives it.

Effective 10 November 2026 · Applies to the Kippu app for iPhone

LocationUsed on your phone to time your walk. Never sent to us.
Trip & profileKept only on your phone.
Label photosRead on your phone. Sent to ChatGPT only if you turn that on.

Who we are

Kippu is published by Groma Cyber Intelligence Ltd (“we”), gromaintel.com. For anything about this policy, email support@gromaintel.com.

What stays on your phone

  • Your profile. The name you give Kippu, where you're staying, your walking pace, safety buffer, allergies and diet, saved places, reminders and display settings are stored on your phone.
  • Location. Only after you allow it, Kippu uses your location while the app is open to time your walk to the station, warn you if you might miss your train and sort places by distance. Distances are worked out on your phone and your location is not sent to us or stored by Kippu.
  • Reminders. Leave and last-train reminders are scheduled by your phone. No server sends them.
  • Timetables. Kyoto's timetables are built into the app, so looking them up sends nothing.
  • Look & walk. Only while you use it, Kippu uses the camera and your phone's motion sensors to draw the walking path over the street ahead. The camera view is shown on screen only: it is never recorded, saved or sent.
  • Label reading. The label scanner reads and translates text with Google ML Kit on your phone. Photos are processed in memory and never saved. Allergies you choose to highlight stay on your phone.

We have no access to any of this. We cannot see, recover or delete it for you, so you stay in control on the phone.

What leaves your phone

  • Train times and service status. Kippu requests Tokyo timetables from Japan's Public Transportation Open Data Center (ODPT). Requests name the line and station, and contain no personal details. Like any internet request, they share your IP address.
  • Place search. When you search for your hotel or a place, the words you type are sent to OpenStreetMap's search service (Nominatim), under the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.
  • Directions. When you tap Directions, Kippu opens Google Maps, Apple Maps or Naver Map with the destination. That app then handles your journey under its own privacy policy.
  • Railway websites. Links to official railway pages open those websites, which apply their own privacy policies.
  • Language packs and ML Kit diagnostics. The first time you read a label in a language, your phone downloads its translation pack from Google. Google's ML Kit also sends Google technical diagnostics (device and app information, device identifiers, performance metrics, input sizes but not content, languages used and error codes). Kippu does not send your photos or label text to Google. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Better results with ChatGPT (off unless you turn it on). The first time you scan a label, Kippu asks whether to turn this on; you can change it any time in the label scanner's settings. If you turn it on, each label photo you scan is sent over an encrypted connection through our server (a Cloudflare Worker) to OpenAI, which reads and translates it and returns the result. Our server passes the photo on without storing it or logging its content, and counts scans against a random ID made by the app (not your name or Apple ID). OpenAI may keep API data for up to 30 days to detect abuse and does not use it to train its models, under the OpenAI Privacy Policy. Cloudflare sees your IP address to deliver the request, under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Your profile and allergies are never sent.
  • Talk mode. Only while you use the microphone in Talk or glasses mode, your iPhone's own speech recognition turns speech into text. Your phone may send that audio to Apple to recognise it, under Apple's privacy policy. The conversation is not saved or sent to us.
  • App Store. Apple handles downloads, updates and crash reports under its own privacy policy. TestFlight testers also share the information Apple's TestFlight collects.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, ad identifiers or tracking across apps and websites.
  • No analytics of our own, and no selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No payments in this version.

Your choices

  • Allow or withdraw location, notifications, camera, motion, microphone and speech recognition at any time in your iPhone's Settings → Kippu. Kippu still works without them, with less help.
  • Leave Better results with ChatGPT off, or turn it off at any time, and label photos stay on your phone.
  • Deleting the app removes everything Kippu stores. There is no copy anywhere else.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under UK or EU GDPR or Californian law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to complain to a data protection authority, such as the UK Information Commissioner's Office. Because Kippu stores your information only on your phone and we don't receive it, you can use these rights directly on your phone. For data Google, Apple or OpenStreetMap handle, contact them. If you have a question, email us.

Children

Kippu is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date. Important changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
