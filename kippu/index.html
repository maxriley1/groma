Know when to leave.

Kippu is your travel buddy for Japan's trains. It times your walk to the station, tells you which train and platform, checks whether you can still reach a sight before it closes, and works back from the last train to your hotel.

Getting started

  1. Set up. On first launch, tell Kippu your name, where you're staying, how fast you walk and how much safety buffer you like. You can change these any time under Me.
  2. Allow location and notifications. Location times your walk to the station. Notifications let Kippu remind you when it's time to go, even with the app closed.
  3. Pick a place. Visit shows which sights you can still reach before last entry or closing. Tap one, then When should we leave? for a live countdown with the train and platform.
  4. Get home. Get home shows the latest moment you can leave and still make every last connection to your hotel.

Questions

Are the train times real?

Kyoto City Subway and Kyoto City Bus 205 use the official published timetables, with platforms and bus bays. Toei Subway in Tokyo uses its open timetable data. Other rides use typical journey times, marked as sample in the app, with links to each railway's official pages. Times are scheduled: delays aren't shown, so allow a buffer and check station boards.

Which cities does it cover?

Kyoto, Osaka and Tokyo, plus popular day trips. Real timetables cover Kyoto and Toei Subway today; more cities will follow.

The countdown or walk time looks wrong

Check your walking pace and safety buffer under Me, and that location access is allowed (iPhone: Settings → Kippu → Location → While Using the App). Indoors or underground, GPS can be inaccurate.

I didn't get a reminder

Allow notifications for Kippu (iPhone: Settings → Kippu → Notifications) and check Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them. Reminders are scheduled on your phone, so they work offline.

Directions open the wrong map app

Kippu opens Google Maps if it's installed, Naver Map in South Korea, and Apple Maps otherwise.

How does label reading work?

Tap the label scanner, point the camera at a Japanese food or product label and Kippu reads and translates it on your phone. It highlights allergens you've told it about. The first scan downloads a language pack (about 30 MB), then it works offline.

How do I delete my data?

Everything Kippu stores is on your phone, so deleting the app removes it completely. See the privacy policy.

Important limits

!

Kippu helps you plan; it isn't an official railway service. Timetables can change and trains can be delayed, so check station boards and announcements. Label translations can be wrong and never mean a product is safe for you: with allergies, ask staff and check the pack.
